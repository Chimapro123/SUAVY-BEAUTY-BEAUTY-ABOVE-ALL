A simple daytime ritual

Make sun care part of your everyday.

A little planning can make it easier to keep sun care in your routine. Always read the directions and warnings on the sunscreen you choose.

01Choose with the label in mindCheck the SPF, broad-spectrum information, ingredients and directions printed on the product.
02Apply as directedFollow the product's instructions for how much to apply and when to reapply.
03Pair with other sun protectionWhen outdoors, consider shade, protective clothing, a hat and sunglasses as part of your sun-care routine.
